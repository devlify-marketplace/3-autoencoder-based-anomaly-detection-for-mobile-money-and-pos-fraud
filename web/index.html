Transaction Anomaly Lab

A normal-only autoencoder learns a compact representation of genuine transactions and ranks unusual rows for review—without training on fraud examples.

● Local research dashboard
!

Proxy benchmark — not Nigerian mobile-money/POS validation. This prototype is trained and evaluated on real European credit-card transactions from the public OpenML benchmark.

The results do not establish performance on Nigerian mobile-money or POS transactions. A review flag means unusual, not confirmed fraud. Do not use this prototype to automatically block payments.
